# block-pusher-scene

Your hand is one sphere, a floating sphere is the other, and you push it around. Zero gravity, a
weight you can tune, air density you can play with.

Worth being precise about what is and isn't borrowed. Bongard's block pushers are one organism of
jointed spheres grown by a genetic regulatory network, and they crawl using ground contact — so the
locomotion does not transfer to drones. What does transfer, and is the stronger idea, is that the
environment shapes the creature: touch a growing agent and a simulated chemical diffuses from the
contact point and changes which genes fire, and over generations agents became more sensitive to it.
That is the brief's "what we put into the simulation would include the movement of the person", and
it is the thing worth building toward.
